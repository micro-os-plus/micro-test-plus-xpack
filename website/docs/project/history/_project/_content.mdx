### February 2021

In February 2021, the initial experimental version was developed, drawing inspiration from node-tap and prioritising maximum simplicity.

### March 2021

During March 2021, as part of a broader project restructuring, the C++ namespace was renamed to `micro_os_plus`.

### April 2022

At the beginning of 2022, a significant reworking of the library commenced, with the objective of enhancing flexibility and supporting multiple test suites. The principal source of inspiration for this revision was [UT](https://boost-ext.github.io/ut/). The v3.x release was subsequently published in April 2022.

### February 2021

In February 2021, the first experimental version, inspired by node-tap,
was created, aiming for maximum simplicity.

### March 2021

In March 2021, part of the project restructuring, the C++ namespace was renamed
`micro_os_plus`.

### April 2022

At the beginning of 2022, a major wre-work was started, to make the library
more flexible and to allow multiple test suites. The new source of inspiration
was [UT](https://boost-ext.github.io/ut/). The v3.x release was published
in April 2022.

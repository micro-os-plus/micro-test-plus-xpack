Detailed Description

template<class T>
struct micro_os_plus::micro_test_plus::detail::comparison_integer< T >

The primary template is the type itself. The character types (char, wchar_t, char8_t, char16_t, and char32_t) are compared as the numbers that they hold, so they are replaced by the integer type of the same size and signedness, which std::cmp_*() accepts; the signedness of char and wchar_t depends on the platform, and is kept.

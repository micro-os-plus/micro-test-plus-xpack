Detailed Description

The functions mt::_and(), mt::_or(), and mt::_not() are the v5.0.1 names of mt::all_of(), mt::any_of(), and mt::negate(). They are deprecated since 5.1.0, and are kept for compatibility with the existing applications; they behave as the functions that replace them.

The names were changed since a leading underscore is not used by the other public names. Unlike the replacements, mt::_and() and mt::_or() take exactly two operands, whereas mt::all_of() and mt::any_of() take two or more.

Deprecated Replacement
mt::_and() mt::all_of()
mt::_or() mt::any_of()
mt::_not() mt::negate()
Example
// Deprecated.
t.expect (mt::_and (mt::eq (compute_answer (), 42),
mt::lt (compute_answer (), 43)));
// Replacement.
t.expect (mt::all_of (mt::eq (compute_answer (), 42),
mt::lt (compute_answer (), 43)));

The overloaded logical operators of the micro_test_plus::operators namespace are also deprecated since 5.1.0, in favour of the same replacement functions.
